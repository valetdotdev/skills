How this page works

The page is static HTML. Every number on it is fetched in your browser, when you open it, from a connector an organization member attached to this site. Nothing is baked in at publish time, and no credential is in the page: calls go to /__valet/mcp/<connector> on this same hostname, and Valet attaches the organization's credential server-side.

Each section calls its own tool and renders its own failure. A connector that is slow, unreachable, or refusing one tool family leaves every other section on the page intact.
